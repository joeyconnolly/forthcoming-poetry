How dates and inclusion work

Exact dates cease to be forthcoming on publication day, in UK time. Month-only dates remain through month end; seasons use March–May, June–August, September–November and December–February. We show the publisher’s precision rather than an invented release day.

Poetry collections, selected and collected poems, anthologies, pamphlets and translations are in scope. Criticism, magazines, routine format reissues and uncertain hybrid works are excluded or held for review. This prototype does not claim comprehensive UK and Irish coverage.
